Privacy Policy

Effective Date: 1 January 2026  |  Last Updated: 1 July 2026

CompliQ by REUDE Technologies Pvt. Ltd. ("CompliQ", "we", "our", or "us") is committed to protecting your personal information. This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, disclose, and safeguard your data when you use our platform.

1. Information We Collect

We collect the following categories of information:

  • Account Information: Name, email address, organisation name, and role provided during registration.
  • Usage Data: Pages visited, features accessed, inspection queries submitted, and session duration.
  • Inspection Data: Drone imagery, thermal scans, telemetry payloads, defect records, and GPS coordinates uploaded to the platform.
  • Device & Technical Data: Browser type, IP address, operating system, and referral URLs.

2. How We Use Your Information

  • To provide, operate, and maintain the CompliQ inspection platform.
  • To authenticate users and manage account access.
  • To process and display inspection results, defect reports, and fleet analytics.
  • To send platform-related communications such as alerts and report notifications.
  • To improve our AI models, platform features, and user experience.
  • To comply with applicable legal obligations.

3. Data Storage & Security

Your data is stored securely using industry-standard encryption at rest and in transit (TLS 1.3). Access is restricted to authorised personnel only. Inspection data is stored in compliance with applicable data localisation requirements.

4. Data Sharing

We do not sell your personal data. We may share data with:

  • Service Providers: Cloud infrastructure, analytics, and support partners operating under strict data processing agreements.
  • Legal Authorities: Where required by law, court order, or regulatory obligation.
  • Business Transfers: In the event of a merger, acquisition, or asset sale, subject to confidentiality obligations.

5. Cookies & Tracking

We use essential cookies to maintain sessions and preferences. We do not use third-party advertising cookies. You may disable cookies in your browser settings, though this may affect platform functionality.

6. Your Rights

Depending on your jurisdiction, you may have the right to:

  • Access, correct, or delete your personal data.
  • Restrict or object to certain processing activities.
  • Request data portability in a machine-readable format.
  • Withdraw consent where processing is consent-based.

To exercise these rights, contact us at cmo_ira@reude.tech.

7. Data Retention

We retain account data for as long as your account is active. Inspection data is retained for a minimum of 3 years for audit and compliance purposes, unless you request earlier deletion.

8. Children's Privacy

CompliQ is an enterprise platform not intended for use by persons under 18 years of age. We do not knowingly collect personal data from minors.

9. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy periodically. We will notify registered users of material changes via email or an in-platform notice. Continued use of the platform after changes constitutes acceptance.
