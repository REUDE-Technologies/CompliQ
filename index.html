CompliQ by REUDE Technologies is an AI-driven renewable energy inspection platform with one mission make the invisible visible, across wind turbines, solar panels and critical energy infrastructure, faster and safer than ever before.

Our renewable energy inspection platform bridges the gap between drone-captured raw imagery and actionable operator intelligence. We combine computer vision, edge AI and structured telemetry pipelines to detect defects across wind turbine blades, solar panels and energy infrastructure at scale, before they become failures.

Autonomous UAV Systems

Fully automated inspection campaigns for wind and solar assets with sub-10mm resolution imagery.

Edge Intelligence

Onboard AI inference for wind blade and solar panel defect detection with < 200ms decision latency at the edge.

Predictive Analytics

Temporal defect progression modelling and maintenance prioritisation across your entire renewable energy portfolio.
